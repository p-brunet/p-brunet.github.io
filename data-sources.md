## LiDAR & Elevation

- [VCGI (Vermont)](https://geodata.vermont.gov/): high-resolution LiDAR tiles (35–70 cm)
- [NRCan HRDEM (datacube STAC)](https://datacube.services.geo.ca/stac/api): 1 m LiDAR-derived DTM/DSM mosaics as cloud-optimized GeoTIFFs (Canada)

## Energy

### Infrastructure

- [ENTSO-E Transparency Platform](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/): European electricity transmission data (generation, load, cross-border flows)
- [AER Data](https://www.aer.ca/data-and-performance-reports/statistical-reports/st60): Alberta Energy Regulator data archive on crude oil production (CH4 monitoring) 
- [OpenStreetMap power lines (Overpass API)](https://overpass-api.de/): transmission line ways, towers and substations
- [IESO Intertie Schedule and Flow](https://reports-public.ieso.ca/public/IntertieScheduleFlow/): hourly/5-min Ontario intertie flows (rolling ~3 months)

## Weather

- [ECCC HRDPS via dynamical.org](https://dynamical.org/): 2.5 km Canadian weather forecasts (0–48 h) as cloud-native Zarr
- [MSC GeoMet OGC API](https://api.weather.gc.ca/): ECCC hourly station observations (climate-hourly)
